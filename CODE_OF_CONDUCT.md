# Community Conduct

Sazan Quickstarts is intended to be a professional, collaborative open-source project.

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, protect private information, and disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that are abusive, deceptive, unsafe, off-topic, or expose sensitive information. Repeated or severe violations may result in participation restrictions.

For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting path documented in `SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
